## Assertion

On the current depth-2 shallow anchor, introducing a small positive warmup helped more than either no warmup or a larger warmup. `WARMUP_RATIO=0.05` improved `val_bpb` by `0.002954` versus the incoming anchor, while `0.1` gave the gain back and `0.5` regressed.

## Falsification

The explicit disproof attempt raised `WARMUP_RATIO` to `0.5`, which should have overturned the hypothesis if a broad or long warmup were beneficial. Instead it finished worse than both the incoming anchor and the same-session zero-warmup control. The closer neighbor test at `0.1` also failed to hold the `0.05` gain, so the surviving claim is not that "more warmup is better," but that a narrow warmup near `5%` may help this anchor.

## Implications

Future schedule work on the shallow anchor should treat `WARMUP_RATIO=0.05` as a promising but still unreproduced improvement. The next follow-up should reproduce `0.0` versus `0.05` directly, or probe a tighter neighborhood such as `0.03-0.07`, rather than assuming that larger positive warmups will continue to help.
