## Assertion

At the current shallow depth-2 anchor, reducing `MATRIX_LR` from `0.04` to `0.03` improved `val_bpb` within the fixed 5-minute budget. Raising it to `0.06` was much worse, and lowering it further to `0.02` preserved most of the gain but gave back a small amount relative to `0.03`, suggesting a local optimum around `0.03`.

## Conditions

- Apple Silicon MLX training stack in the repository default setup
- Incoming anchor before this session: commit `5af9be75399cef8d24756f0faa6f901ec44d4f54`
- `DEPTH=2`, `TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE=2**15`, `DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE=16`, `ASPECT_RATIO=64`, `HEAD_DIM=128`, `WINDOW_PATTERN="SSSL"`, and AdamW betas/weight decay unchanged
- Peak memory stayed flat at `18.6 GB` across all four runs, and completed steps stayed in the `2211-2216` range

## Falsification

The explicit falsification run increased `MATRIX_LR` to `0.06`, which would have supported the opposite story if a more aggressive matrix learning rate were beneficial. Instead it regressed sharply to `1.498786`, worse than both the incumbent anchor and the control rerun, so the high-LR side of the hypothesis was decisively rejected.

## Implications

The matrix learning-rate surface is not flat around the shallow anchor: modestly reducing it matters, but pushing lower to `0.02` does not continue the improvement. The next optimizer-focused follow-up should keep `MATRIX_LR=0.03` fixed and probe adjacent families such as `EMBEDDING_LR`, `UNEMBEDDING_LR`, or Adam betas from this new best commit.
