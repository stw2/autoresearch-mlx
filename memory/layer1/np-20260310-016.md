## Assertion

At the current shallow depth-2 anchor, decaying all the way to zero does not look optimal, but the benefit from keeping a nonzero learning-rate floor appears narrow and low-confidence. `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.05` improved `val_bpb` by `0.001368`, while `0.1` gave the gain back and the large-floor falsification at `0.5` failed badly.

## Falsification

The explicit disproof attempt raised the learning-rate floor to `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.5`, which would have overturned the hypothesis if keeping a high late-training LR were beneficial. Instead it regressed sharply to `1.464666`, much worse than both the incoming anchor and the noisy control rerun. The closer neighbor test at `0.1` also failed to hold the gain from `0.05`, so the surviving claim is only that a very small floor may help, not that larger nonzero floors are broadly good.

## Implications

Future schedule work on this shallow anchor should treat `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.05` as a promising but unsettled local improvement that needs reproduction. The next follow-up should rerun `0.0` versus `0.05` directly, or probe a tighter neighborhood such as `0.02-0.08`, rather than pushing the floor higher.
