## Assertion

At the current depth-2 shallow anchor with `MATRIX_LR=0.03`, increasing AdamW momentum above the incumbent `ADAM_BETAS=(0.8, 0.95)` did not produce a reliable improvement in `val_bpb`. A small increase to `beta1=0.85` was effectively tied with the control, while stronger momentum at `beta1=0.9` and the stickier pair `(0.9, 0.99)` both regressed.

## Falsification

The original hypothesis predicted that a modest beta increase should beat the control while a very sticky setting would fail. The explicit high-momentum falsification run at `ADAM_BETAS=(0.9, 0.99)` failed hard, regressing by `0.016527 bpb`. The closer disproof attempt at `ADAM_BETAS=(0.85, 0.95)` came closest to overturning the negative conclusion, but it still finished `0.000076 bpb` worse than the control, which is below the repo's stated noise threshold and therefore not strong evidence of improvement.

## Implications

The new control rerun improves the shallow anchor slightly, but this session does not justify changing the AdamW beta defaults. Future optimizer work should look elsewhere first, or treat any beta1 tuning around `0.8-0.85` as a low-confidence, reproduction-dependent question rather than a settled gain.
