## Assertion

Re-testing embedding learning rate on the current shallow anchor with `WARMUP_RATIO=0.02` and `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.02` did not reproduce any improvement over the inherited best run. Among the tested values, `EMBEDDING_LR=0.8` still gave the best result in this session, while `0.7` finished only `0.000380 bpb` worse and `0.6`/`0.9` were clearly worse. The practical conclusion is a noisy `0.7-0.8` band, not a new better default.

## Falsification

The planned disproof cases pushed embedding LR to both sides of the proposed plateau. If the updated schedule wanted a meaningfully lower or higher embedding step size, `0.6` or `0.9` should have beaten the `0.8` control. Instead both regressed clearly, at `1.413893` and `1.414275`. The only remaining positive signal was the `0.7` run at `1.412282`, but that still missed the `0.8` control by `0.000380 bpb`, which is below the stated noise floor and therefore not enough to claim a better optimum.

## Implications

Future sessions should not spend more budget trying to flip the current anchor from `EMBEDDING_LR=0.8` to `0.7` based on a single near-tie. If embedding LR is revisited again, it should either be a pure reproducibility check of `0.7` versus `0.8` with multiple reruns or be paired with a larger architectural or optimizer change that could move the plateau more substantially.
