## Assertion

Re-testing warmdown under the current shallow anchor (`WARMUP_RATIO=0.02`, `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.02`) did not validate the hypothesis that a shorter schedule helps. `WARMDOWN_RATIO=0.25` regressed against both the inherited anchor and the same-session `0.5` control, while a steeper `0.75` warmdown only partially recovered and still failed to beat the inherited anchor. The safe conclusion is that the positive `0.25` hypothesis is refuted and the warmdown axis remains noisy.

## Falsification

The designed disproof case removed warmdown entirely (`WARMDOWN_RATIO=0.0`). If the original hypothesis were right that the current schedule should be materially flatter, the no-warmdown run should have remained competitive. Instead it collapsed to `1.494258`, much worse than every other run. The direct target probe at `0.25` then also lost to the same-session `0.5` control, which is enough to reject the claim that shortening warmdown helps under the updated warmup and LR-floor settings.

## Implications

Future sessions should not treat `WARMDOWN_RATIO=0.25` as promising on this anchor. The one interesting residual signal is that `0.75` beat the same-session `0.5` control while still missing the inherited anchor, so any follow-up should be framed as a reproducibility check around `0.5-0.75`, not as evidence that a shorter warmdown is beneficial.
