## Assertion

The previous single-run result at `EMBEDDING_LR=0.8` did not reproduce cleanly enough to treat it as a settled local optimum. In this follow-up sweep, `0.6` and `0.9` were clearly worse than the incoming `0.8` anchor, but `0.7` finished only `0.000301 bpb` behind it, so the practical conclusion is a low-confidence plateau across `0.7-0.8` with `0.9` on the losing side.

## Falsification

The strong version of the hypothesis required `EMBEDDING_LR=0.8` to reproduce as the best point in the neighborhood. The control rerun at the same `0.8` setting failed that test immediately, regressing to `1.423273`. The lower-side disproof attempt at `0.6` also failed to beat the anchor, but the near-tie at `0.7` means the surviving evidence only supports a broad `0.7-0.8` region, not a sharp or reproducible optimum at exactly `0.8`.

## Implications

Treat `EMBEDDING_LR=0.8` as an unsettled low-confidence anchor rather than as a locked-in improvement. Follow-up optimizer work should either reproduce `0.7` versus `0.8` directly with multiple reruns, or move on to new axes such as `UNEMBEDDING_LR` while keeping the embedding learning rate in the `0.7-0.8` band and avoiding `0.9`.
