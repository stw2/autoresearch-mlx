## Falsification

The session began from the opposite claim that lower weight decay should help, so the first real falsification of the incumbent was the `WEIGHT_DECAY=0.1` run. If `0.2` were over-regularizing the shallow anchor, `0.1` should have improved on `1.431264`; instead it regressed to `1.437937`. The zero-decay endpoint failed much harder at `1.492884`, while the opposite-direction check at `0.4` also regressed, leaving the incumbent in the middle as the best tested value.

## Implications

For the current shallow anchor, the existing `WEIGHT_DECAY=0.2` should be treated as provisionally stable. Follow-up sessions should search other optimizer axes or schedules before revisiting weight decay, unless another architectural change materially alters the regularization tradeoff.
