## Assertion

The previous single-run win for `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.05` did not reproduce on the current shallow anchor. In a direct follow-up sweep, neither the smaller floors `0.03` and `0.02` nor the explicit zero-floor falsification run beat the incoming anchor commit, so there is no reliable evidence that a small warmdown floor helps this setup.

## Falsification

The explicit disproof attempt was the zero-floor rerun at `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.0`, which would have overturned the original claim if decaying all the way to zero clearly beat the anchor or separated from the small-floor neighborhood. It did not: `0.0` finished `0.002934 bpb` worse than the anchor, and the nearby `0.03` run was effectively tied with it while still missing the anchor. That leaves the previous `0.05` gain unsupported and refutes the idea that a smaller nonzero floor reliably recovers it.

## Implications

Treat the earlier `FINAL_LR_FRAC=0.05` improvement as noise rather than as a new default. Future sessions should keep the anchor at `7aa0953d0a1bb12bff94507f24024b8ec88b8c4d` and spend search budget on other axes unless a later architectural change gives a fresh reason to revisit late-training LR floors.
